01

Who this app is for

The Udaipur Toloba app is an internal tool for managing dues and payments within Udaipur Toloba, a private community organization. It is not a public service — every person on it is a verified member or administrator of this one organization, and the app is not intended for use outside it.

02

Information we hold

Because this app tracks membership and financial records, it holds the following about each member:

Identity

Full name, ITS membership ID, mohallah / area, and designation within the organization.

Contact

Phone number, used for member identification and contact by administrators.

Photo

A profile photograph, used for member identification within the app.

Financial records

Dues assigned, payments recorded, and transaction history tied to your membership.

Device token

A push-notification token, used only to deliver in-app alerts about your dues and payments.

We do not collect location data, contacts, browsing history, or any information unrelated to membership and dues tracking. The app has no advertising, no analytics tracking, and no third-party marketing integrations.

03

How accounts work

There is no public sign-up. Every account is created by an organization administrator, and login credentials are handed to members directly, outside the app. If you did not receive credentials from an administrator, you cannot obtain an account.

04

How we use it

  • Tracking dues. Your name, ID, and financial records are used to calculate what you owe and what you've paid.
  • Recording payments. When an administrator or treasurer logs a payment, it's attached to your record.
  • Notifying you. Push notifications tell you when a payment is received or needs approval — nothing promotional.
  • Reporting. Aggregated, area-wise summaries help administrators understand collection status. These reports are only visible to authorized administrators, secretaries, treasurers, and area in-charges.
05

Who else sees it

We don't sell or share your data with advertisers. A small number of infrastructure providers process it on our behalf, strictly to run the app:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseHosts the database and stores profile photos securely.
Apple / Google push servicesDeliver push notifications to your device via your device token.

None of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

06

Storage & security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to member and financial data is restricted to signed-in administrators and staff roles, enforced on every request. Photos are stored in a private bucket and only ever served through time-limited, signed links.

07

Retention & deletion

Your record is kept for as long as you remain a member, since it forms part of the organization's financial history. If you'd like your account deactivated or your data deleted, contact your organization administrator directly — they can deactivate your access immediately or permanently remove your record on request.

There's no self-service delete button in the app by design: these are official financial records, and their removal needs administrative sign-off, the same way you couldn't delete your own record from a company payroll system.
08

Age of our members

Membership in Udaipur Toloba isn't restricted by age, so the app's members can include minors as well as adults. This doesn't change how accounts work: every account, regardless of the member's age, is created and managed by an organization administrator — nobody, including a minor, can self-register (see "How accounts work" above). A parent or guardian with questions about a minor member's data, or a request to have it removed, can reach the organization administrator the same way any member would.

09
